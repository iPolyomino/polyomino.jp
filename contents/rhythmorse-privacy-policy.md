# Rhythmorse Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Polyomino ("we," "us," or "our") provides Rhythmorse, a Morse code practice application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app, including its advertising features.

## Practice Data and App Settings

Rhythmorse does not require an account. Practice messages and preferences, such as practice speed and sound settings, are stored on your device. Tap timing is processed on your device to display and evaluate your Morse code practice.

We do not send your practice messages or tap timing records to our own servers or intentionally provide them to the advertising SDK. Advertising-related interaction data is described separately below.

Android may back up or transfer app settings according to your device and backup settings. Such backups are handled by your backup provider.

## Advertising and Automatically Collected Information

Rhythmorse displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. This SDK automatically collects and shares information with Google for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Depending on SDK configuration, device settings, and available identifiers, this may include:

- Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location.
- App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps, and ad views.
- Performance and diagnostic information about the app and SDK.
- Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, app set ID, and other identifiers where applicable.

Google may use this information to deliver and measure advertisements, personalize advertising where permitted, maintain its services, and detect invalid traffic or abuse. Non-personalized advertising may still involve data collection for these purposes.

For more information, see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google advertising technologies and controls](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure)

## Your Choices

You can change your practice messages and preferences within Rhythmorse. You can remove locally stored app data using Android's app storage settings or by uninstalling the app. Backup copies may remain subject to your backup provider's settings and retention practices.

You can manage, reset, or delete your advertising ID through the advertising privacy controls in your Android device settings, where available. The available controls and their location vary by Android version and device. These choices do not necessarily stop advertisements or all advertising-related data collection.

Google also provides controls described in its privacy and advertising policies linked above.

## Data Retention and Deletion

App preferences remain on your device until changed or removed. Practice tap records are held temporarily in app memory and reset during practice.

Google determines the retention and deletion of information collected through its advertising services under its own policies. Clearing Rhythmorse's local data does not automatically delete information previously received by Google.

If you contact us, we use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry and retain it only as needed to handle the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

## Security and International Processing

Google states that data collected by the advertising SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Google may process advertising-related information outside your country of residence, as described in its Privacy Policy.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. We will post the revised policy on the [Rhythmorse support page](https://polyomino.jp/support/rhythmorse) and update the date above.
