# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

Polyomino (“the Developer”) handles information relating to users of the iOS app “TRPG DICE” (“the App”) as described below.

## 1. Information stored on your device

The App stores dice roll history, including formulas, results, and timestamps, as well as settings such as purchase status, on your device. This information is used to display your history and provide purchased features.

The Developer does not transmit your dice roll history to a server operated by the Developer. You can delete saved history from the App’s history screen.

## 2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements.

In connection with advertising, Google and advertising service providers may collect and process information such as your IP address, approximate location inferred from your IP address, device or app identifiers other than IDFA, ad impressions and interactions, crash information, and performance information. This information may be used to deliver and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and maintain and improve services.

The App does not request permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework and does not use the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA). This does not mean that no data is collected or processed in connection with advertising.

For details about Google’s handling of information, please see:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en)

## 3. Advertising consent and choices

Where required by applicable regional regulations, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide information and present consent or choice forms relating to the use of data for advertising.

When available, you can review and change your choices using “Ad privacy settings” on the App’s history screen. This option appears when required. The App requests advertisements only when UMP indicates that ad requests are permitted.

## 4. In-app purchases

Apple processes the App’s in-app purchases and payments. The App uses purchase information provided by Apple to enable purchased features and restore purchases. The Developer does not receive payment details such as your credit card number.

For details about Apple’s handling of information, please see the [Apple Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/en-ww/).

## 5. Retention and deletion

History stored on your device is removed when you delete it within the App or when the App’s history limit is reached. You can also remove local app data by deleting the App. Data included in operating system backups may be retained or restored according to Apple’s systems and your settings.

For retention periods and deletion options relating to information processed by Google or Apple, please refer to their respective policies.
